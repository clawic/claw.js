# ClawJS Safety Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-18

This policy summarizes the safety boundary enforced by
`docs/regulated-domain-safety.md`,
`docs/adr/0026-regulated-domain-safety-liability-boundary.md`, and
`packages/clawjs-core/src/regulated-domain-safety.ts`.

## English

### Allowed sensitive use

Sensitive domains may be used for local recordkeeping, search, extraction,
factual summary, questions to review, gaps and provenance, non-final drafts,
and preparation for human or professional review.

### Blocked sensitive use

ClawJS must not make final regulated decisions, diagnose or treat, provide
therapy or crisis counseling, provide final legal/financial advice, decide
credit, insurance, employment, education, public benefits, emergency response,
or physical safety, or autonomously submit regulated filings.

### Required review

External sensitive actions, sensitive export/share, remote/sync, provider use,
support diagnostics, publication, signing, payment, purchase, filing, or
submission require explicit contextual review. Some actions remain blocked even
with approval.

### Labels

Sensitive outputs must preserve labels equivalent to: draft, not professional
advice, human review required, sources and gaps required, regulated domain, and
decision effect.

## Espanol

### Uso sensible permitido

Los dominios sensibles pueden usarse para registro local, busqueda, extraccion,
resumen factual, preguntas para revisar, gaps y procedencia, borradores no
finales y preparacion para revision humana o profesional.

### Uso sensible bloqueado

ClawJS no debe tomar decisiones reguladas finales, diagnosticar o tratar,
ofrecer terapia o apoyo de crisis, dar consejo legal/financiero final, decidir
credito, seguros, empleo, educacion, ayudas publicas, emergencias, seguridad
fisica ni presentar filings regulados autonomamente.

### Revision requerida

Acciones externas sensibles, export/share sensible, remoto/sync, proveedores,
diagnosticos de soporte, publicacion, firma, pago, compra, filing o submission
requieren revision contextual explicita. Algunas acciones siguen bloqueadas
incluso con aprobacion.

### Etiquetas

Los outputs sensibles deben conservar etiquetas equivalentes a: borrador, no es
asesoramiento profesional, revision humana requerida, fuentes y gaps
requeridos, dominio regulado y efecto de decision.
