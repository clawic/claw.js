Social Media

Your Content Calendar, Drafted and Reviewed

AI agents can prepare posts, scheduling plans, reply drafts, and engagement summaries. Publishing remains explicit and approval-gated.

social.ts 
// Schedule a post
await claw.social.schedule({
  platform: "instagram",
  content: "Launch day!",
  time: "9:00 AM",
});

// Get analytics
const stats = await claw
  .social.analytics(
    "instagram"
  );

// Prepare a reply draft
await claw.social
  .draftReply({
    platforms: ["x", "ig"]
  });
