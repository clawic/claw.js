Scheduling

AI Agents Own Your Calendar

Scheduler agents find optimal meeting times, detect conflicts, auto-book prep slots, and keep your week balanced. A full calendar where every gap is an opportunity for an agent.

calendar.ts 
// Schedule a meeting
await claw.calendar.schedule({
  title: "Product Demo",
  duration: "1h",
  attendees: ["alex"],
});

// Find optimal time
const slots = await claw
  .calendar.findSlots({
    duration: "30m",
    prefer: "morning"
  });

// Check conflicts
const conflicts = await claw
  .calendar
  .conflicts("today");
