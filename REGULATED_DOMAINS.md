# ClawJS Regulated Domains

Last updated: 2026-05-18

The canonical executable registry is
`packages/clawjs-core/src/regulated-domain-safety.ts`.

## Covered domains

- health
- mental health
- reproductive and intimate data
- pharma
- finance
- banking
- insurance
- legal
- HR and employment
- education
- government and public services
- housing and real estate
- compliance and GRC
- identity
- security
- billing and payments
- labs and research
- IoT and physical actions
- vehicles and transport
- minors

## English

For each domain, ClawJS allows local recordkeeping, search, extraction,
factual summary, questions to review, gaps/provenance, non-final drafts, and
preparation for human or professional review. ClawJS blocks final regulated
decisions and requires labels, disclaimers, and explicit review for sensitive
external actions, exports, remote/sync, and provider use.

## Espanol

Para cada dominio, ClawJS permite registro local, busqueda, extraccion,
resumen factual, dudas para revisar, gaps/procedencia, borradores no finales y
preparacion para revision humana o profesional. ClawJS bloquea decisiones
reguladas finales y exige etiquetas, disclaimers y revision explicita para
acciones externas sensibles, exports, remoto/sync y uso de proveedores.
